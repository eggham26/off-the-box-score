Off the Box Score is a season-long student project. Every number is collected from public NBA game data and play-by-play, then recalculated each morning. The picks come from Claude, an AI model, made before tip-off and never changed.
Definitions: a run is 8+ straight points while the other team scores none. Clutch is the last 2:00 of the 4th quarter or overtime with the margin at 5 or less. A key player averages 24+ minutes a game. Not affiliated with the NBA or any team.
